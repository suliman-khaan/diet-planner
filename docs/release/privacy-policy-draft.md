## Summary

- The app works without an account. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
- We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.
- Health details (weight, height, age, allergies, health conditions, what you eat) never leave your phone unless you
  export or share them yourself.

## What the app stores on your phone

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Goal, birth year, sex, height, weight, activity level, health conditions you tick | To decide whether automatic plans are safe for you and to estimate your calorie target |
| Diet type, allergies, excluded foods | To keep foods you cannot eat out of your plans |
| Meal times, Ramadan dates, cooking richness | To build your plans and reminders |
| Plans, food you log, weight entries, shopping list | So you can see your day and your progress |
| App settings and your consent choices | To remember your choices |

This data is stored in a database on your phone. Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account if you have
backup turned on; on Android 12 and later such backups are end-to-end encrypted when your phone has a screen lock.

## What leaves your phone

- **Nothing, by default.**
- **Exports:** when you tap Export, the app creates a file and opens Android's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice.
- **Feedback and missing-food requests:** open your own email app with a message you can edit before sending.
- **Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports:** only if you turn them on in Privacy & Data. They never include
  your weight, height, age, allergies, health conditions, foods or search text. [Owner: name the provider, e.g.
  Firebase, once it is set up; until then the app sends nothing.]

## Your choices

- Turn statistics and crash reports on or off at any time in **More → Privacy & data**.
- **Export** all your data as a file.
- **Delete all** data from the phone in **More → Privacy & data**. This cannot be undone.
- Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from your phone (Android backups follow your Google account settings).

## Children

Automatic meal plans are only for adults aged 18 and over. Younger users can log food but get no calorie target.

## Health notice

Calories and nutrients are estimates. The app does not give medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
